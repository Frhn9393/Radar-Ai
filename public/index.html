AUDIT & METODOLOGI BACKTEST KUANTITATIF (IDX)

Pengujian historis multi-tahun sistem algoritma analisa saham & foreign flow

1. Algoritma Pelacakan Aliran Asing (Foreign Flow Engine)

Sistem menggunakan perpaduan Close Location Value (CLV), Volume-Price Spread Analysis (VPA), dan Foreign Flow Pressure Index (FFPI). Setiap fraksi pergerakan harga diuji terhadap rasio partisipasi modal asing (35% - 46% untuk saham tier perbankan LQ45).

CLV = ((Close - Low) - (High - Close)) / (High - Low)
FFPI = TotalTurnover * ForeignWeight * (0.65 * CLV + 0.35 * tanh(Chg% / 2.5))
Foreign VWAP = Sum(Close * NetForeignVal) / Sum(NetForeignVol * 100)
